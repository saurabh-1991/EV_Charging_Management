# Product Requirements

## Objective
Replace manual EV charging register operations with a mobile-first system for residents and society admins.

## Primary Personas

- Resident: books slot, records charging session, checks bill, submits payment reference.
- Admin/Staff: manages rates, verifies payments, tracks usage, generates monthly statements.

## MVP Scope

- OTP login with role-based access
- Vehicle registration
- Charging session logging with meter start/end
- Auto unit and amount calculation
- Slot booking and availability
- Monthly bills with downloadable invoice
- Low-cost digital payment flow (UPI/bank transfer + transaction reference)
- Admin payment verification workflow

## Out of Scope (Initial Launch)

- Direct hardware meter integration
- Automated payment gateway settlement
- Online card/netbanking processing
